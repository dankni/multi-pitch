Tindeq Arcade

Connect a Tindeq device via bluetooth. Pull the required force to climb the walls

Set your max pull under the cog. Leave it hanging free while it connects, so it zeroes. It needs Chrome or Edge on Android or a computer. Unfortunatly iPhone's don't support this via a browser. You can play by holding the screen instead.

This browser can't connect to a Progressor.

Not connected

  1. Switch the Progressor on with its button.
  2. Hang it with nothing pulling on it, so it zeroes at 0 kg.
  3. Tap Connect and choose the Progressor in the list.
  4. Pull on it and watch the kg move.

Not in the list? Check Bluetooth is on, and that the Tindeq app isn't connected to it - it talks to one app at a time. It switches itself off after 10 minutes without use.
