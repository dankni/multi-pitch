About this application

A counter for a day of trad climbing. Tap the grade of the routes completed. Saving keeps the count, the hardest grade, a rating and a note. Everything is stored on this device only – nothing is uploaded anywhere.

You can turn on "Ignore UKC data for this date" when the day is in your UKC logbook as well, and it is counted from here rather than twice. Alternatively, just add it to UKC and import your whole logbook here.

UKC logbook

Download the CSV from your UKC logbook (DLOG format) and upload it here. Your trad and sport climbs are then added to the charts and session logs. Any data is stored on this device only. Uploading a CSV replaces the last one, so always download all data. Your CSV is never uploaded anywhere.
