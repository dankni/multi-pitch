    About this application

    A counter for trad climbing.

    If you turn on "Merge with UKC" when the day is also in your UKC logbook, the two are merged into one session: the time and note from here (UKC's note if you leave yours blank), and the trad climbs from whichever has more. Sport and bouldering logged on UKC that day still count. If you don't use the toggle, it counts anything on the same day from UKC logs as separate sessions / climbs.

    UKC logbook

    Download the CSV from your UKC logbook (DLOG format) and upload it here. Your trad and sport climbs are then added to the charts and session logs. Any data is stored on this device only. Uploading a CSV replaces the last one, so always download all data. Your CSV is never uploaded anywhere.
