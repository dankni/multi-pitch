Tabata

Short, hard efforts with short rests. The classic is 20 seconds of work and 10 seconds of rest, eight times: four minutes in all.

Set the work, the rest and the number of sets before you start. The screen goes green while you work, and red with a beep a second for the last three seconds of each rest. Your settings are remembered for next time.
