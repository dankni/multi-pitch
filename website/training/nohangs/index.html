Sub-max "No Hangs"

Emil Abrahamsen's daily no hang routine. Every rep is a 10 second hang and 20 seconds rest. Feet remain on the floor at all times. Exertion should be around 20% of max. Use an edge that is comfortable. Typically between 15mm and 30mm.
