    About this application

    Track your session at an indoor wall. Sessions are combined on the progress page.

    Tap a grade to log it. Minus fixes mistaps. "Show recent logs" can be used if you forget what you have tracked. You can add a title, notes and a star rating to your session when you finish.

    The wrench beside a saved session reopens it, so a session can be added to or corrected later. You can also connect a heart rate monitor, which will log your heart rate alongside the climbs you tap. A Bangle.js 2 watch will let you log climbs from your watch as well.

    By default, everything is kept on your own device using local storage (not uploaded). You can create a free account to back up your data to the cloud and share it between devices.
