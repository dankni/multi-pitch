Training Apps

Free climbing training apps. They all run in the browser, work offline once loaded and keep everything on your own device.

  • Rock Ring Trainer

    The Metolius ten minute rock ring workout, timed and read out as you go. Easy, original and hard plans, and a log of past sessions.

  • Circuit Timer & Lap Counter

    A stopwatch with a lap button. Tap it each time you finish a circuit and it keeps your total time and the number of laps done.

  • Gilford Wall Tick List

    A digital version of the paper tick list for the 24 routes at the Gilford wall. Tap the climbs you got up and save the session.

  • Endurance

    Similar to Stefano Ghisolfi's so called doubles. Endurance or Power Endurance options with configuration.

  • Climbing Twister

    Voice controlled endurance training, originally built for my loft home wall but can be configured to any spray wall. Say "next" and it calls out the next limb and hold colour.
