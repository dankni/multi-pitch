About

I made this as a simple tool to track my climbing progress. It will be free forever for anyone to use. All the tools run in the browser, work offline once loaded and keep everything on your own device using local storage. You can create a free account to back up your data to the cloud and share it between devices.

I built the original version by hand but have now used AI to add new features. It's not perfect but runs more smoothly than some paid climbing apps (looking at you Crimpd, with sound out of sync with the timer).

Add the apps to your home screen: in Safari, tap Share then Add to Home Screen .

Add the apps to your home screen: in Chrome, tap the menu ⋮ then Add to Home screen or Install app.

You can also save them to your phone's homescreen and they will load offline.
