Training Apps

  • Rock Rings

    The Metolius 10 min rock ring workout.

  • Circuit Session

    A stopwatch with a lap button.

  • Gilford Wall

    Gilford wall route tick list.

  • Gym Session

    Track a bouldering gym session.

  • Endurance

    Endurance system similar to Stefano Ghisolfi's so called doubles.

  • Climbing Twister

    Voice controlled endurance training, calls out random limbs and hold colours.

  • Outside Climbing

    Log a day of trad climbing or import logs from UKC.

About

I made this as a simple tool to track my climbing progress. It will be free forever for anyone to use. All the tools run in the browser, work offline once loaded and keep everything on your own device using local storage. You can also save them to your phone's homescreen and they will load offline.

I built the original version by hand but have now used AI to add new features. It's not perfect but runs more smoothly than some paid climbing apps (looking at you Crimpd, with sound out of sync with the timer).

Feedback and suggested features welcome. If you enjoy it I'd love to hear. admin@multi-pitch.com
